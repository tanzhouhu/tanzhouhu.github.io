01 About

Curious about intelligence

I am Zhouhu Tan (谭周湖), an undergraduate majoring in Electronic and Information Engineering at Sichuan University. My research interests center on Large Language Models,Graph Neural Networks, and AI for Science.

I also keep a broad eye on reinforcement learning, computer vision, and embodied AI. Currently, I am actively involved in undergraduate research under the supervision of Associate Professor Wei Ju(琚玮) from the School of Artificial Intelligence, Sichuan University, and collaborate with Dr. Hengchuang Yin(尹衡闯) from the Chinese Academy of Sciences on related research projects.
